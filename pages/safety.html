Safety & quality

Prepared travel builds confident experiences.

WILD plans academic, professional and travel experiences with clear information, verified partners and local support before, during and after arrival.

See our standards ↓

Our approach

Safety is part of the program design.

We work with participants, universities and local partners to identify practical needs early and make responsibilities clear.

Host verification

We review host organisations, roles, supervision and suitability before participant matching.

Preparation

Participants receive pre-departure information, orientation and guidance for local context.

Local support

A local point of contact helps coordinate arrival, communication and practical support.

Accommodation & transport

Arrangements are considered in relation to the program, location and participant needs.

Safeguarding

We expect respectful conduct, appropriate supervision and clear routes for raising concerns.

Incident response

Potential incidents are escalated through agreed contacts and documented response procedures.

Before you travel

Plan with official information.

Travel requirements depend on your nationality, destination and activity. Before departure, check current advice from your government, Uganda’s official authorities and qualified health professionals.

Ask WILD about preparation →

Visa and entry rules

Confirm visa, passport validity and entry requirements for your circumstances well in advance.

Insurance

Arrange appropriate travel, medical and activity cover. WILD does not replace personal insurance.

Health preparation

Seek individual advice from a qualified travel-health professional about vaccinations, medication and health needs.

Emergency contacts

Keep your insurer, university, embassy or consular contact and WILD’s local contact details accessible.

Professional boundaries

Learning must remain within your qualifications.

For medical and health-related programs, WILD does not authorise participants to perform activities beyond their legal qualifications, permissions, training or supervision requirements. Hosts define the permitted scope of participation.

Travel responsibly

Respect people, places and context.

Responsible travel means listening, following local guidance, protecting privacy and treating hosts and communities as partners rather than attractions.
