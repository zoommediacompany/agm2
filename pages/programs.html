Six ways to travel

Find your path.

Each expedition is thoughtfully paced, locally guided and built around real encounters.

◎

Wildlife Safaris

Gorillas, chimpanzees and savannah species. Track them with local guides who know their patterns and habitats intimately.

✦

Photography

Documentary and wildlife photography led by local photographers. We slow down to capture stories that matter.

↗

Adventure

Mountain adventures in the Rwenzori and Virunga ranges. Hiking, trekking and high-altitude experiences.

✦

Conservation

Field research and impact programs alongside conservation partners protecting wildlife and ecosystems.

◎

Culture

Cultural immersion through food, history and community connections. Engage with local families, artisans and traditions.

↗

Private Journeys

Bespoke expeditions for individuals. We design custom itineraries around your interests, timeline and travel style.
