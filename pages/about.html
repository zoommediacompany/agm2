About WILD

Connecting Africa with the world.

WILD connects international students, universities, professionals, researchers and travellers with trusted African institutions, organisations and experiences.

Start a conversation →

Why WILD exists

Making meaningful access possible.

Africa is not a single destination or a single story. WILD is building the trusted connection layer that helps people approach the continent with curiosity, preparation and purpose.

We help turn academic goals, professional interests and personal curiosity into experiences with real local context, clear expectations and responsible support.

Our point of view

Professional access. Local context. Human connection.

01

Access with purpose

We create routes into academic, professional and cultural experiences that have a reason behind them.

02

Relationships over transactions

Participants, universities, hosts and communities are partners in the experience.

03

Trust is designed

Preparation, verification, supervision and local support are part of the work, not afterthoughts.

Our first chapter

Start in Uganda. Build for Africa.

Uganda is WILD’s launch country and our operating foundation. We are focused on building credible local relationships here before expanding to additional destinations.

That means being specific about what is available, honest about what is coming next, and committed to experiences that benefit from local knowledge.
