Safety & standards

Prepared travel starts with clear responsibilities.

Requirements vary by destination, activity and participant. Discuss the confirmed arrangements for your enquiry, consult official guidance and seek qualified advice where appropriate.

Review preparation topics ↓

Before you commit

Confirm the details that matter.

Use these topics to guide planning. Specific responsibilities and arrangements must be discussed and confirmed for each journey or program.

Activity & supervision

Confirm the participant role, activity limits, qualifications and supervision with the relevant host and institution.

Travel documents

Check passport, visa and entry requirements with official authorities for your circumstances.

Health & insurance

Seek qualified health advice and arrange appropriate cover. WILD does not provide medical or insurance advice.

Accommodation & transport

Confirm whether these are included, who arranges them, and what costs or conditions apply.

Consent & conduct

Respect privacy, follow local guidance and obtain consent before photographing or sharing personal stories.

Contacts & escalation

Before departure, agree the relevant contacts and how concerns should be raised for your specific program.

Before you travel

Plan with official information.

Travel requirements depend on your nationality, destination and activity. Before departure, check current advice from your government, Uganda’s official authorities and qualified health professionals.

Ask WILD about preparation →

Visa and entry rules

Confirm visa, passport validity and entry requirements for your circumstances well in advance.

Insurance

Arrange appropriate travel, medical and activity cover. WILD does not replace personal insurance.

Health preparation

Seek individual advice from a qualified travel-health professional about vaccinations, medication and health needs.

Emergency contacts

Keep your insurer, university, embassy or consular contact and WILD’s local contact details accessible.

Professional boundaries

Learning must remain within your qualifications.

For medical and health-related programs, WILD does not authorise participants to perform activities beyond their legal qualifications, permissions, training or supervision requirements. Hosts define the permitted scope of participation.

Travel responsibly

Respect people, places and context.

Responsible travel means listening, following local guidance, protecting privacy and treating hosts and communities as partners rather than attractions.
