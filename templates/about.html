Why WILD exists

Make meaningful access easier to navigate.

Africa is not one destination or one story. It is a continent of people, institutions, landscapes, knowledge and opportunity.

Finding the right route takes relationships, local knowledge, preparation and clear expectations. Vild helps people explore those possibilities without treating access as a promise before the details are confirmed.

Our role is to help make the conversation clearer: understand what you hope to do, identify the questions that matter, and explore whether a suitable connection or arrangement can be made.

The WILD point of view

The way we connect matters.

Access should create the conditions for better understanding, not turn people or places into products. These principles shape how we approach every enquiry.

01

Start with purpose

Know why you are coming.

Begin with a clear reason to travel, learn, research or connect. Good planning starts with listening to that purpose.

02

Build relationships

People are partners.

Local organisations and communities have agency and expertise. Treat them as partners, never as products or attractions.

03

Be clear

Trust needs honesty.

Set out what is known, what still needs agreement and who is responsible before expectations become commitments.

Our beginning

Uganda first. Build with care.

Uganda is Vild’s first operating focus. We aim to build strong local relationships here before expanding into additional countries.

That means describing opportunities carefully, confirming practical details individually and not presenting future plans as services already available.

What we help with

What we do not assume

An enquiry does not guarantee a host, permit, placement, itinerary or booking. Availability, suitability, responsibilities and costs must be discussed and confirmed.
