Our story

Africa has extraordinary possibilities.
Access has always been the challenge.

Africa is home to remarkable wildlife, extraordinary landscapes, exceptional knowledge, vibrant cultures and institutions with the capacity to contribute to the world.

Yet discovering the right experience, finding trusted local expertise and coordinating everything required to make an opportunity happen can be complicated.

We believe there is a better way. VILD Africa Access was conceived around a simple idea: connect international ambition with African capability through a company built on trust, local knowledge and professional execution.

We bring together carefully selected partners, distinctive experiences and coordinated services to make discovering and accessing Africa more straightforward.

From a private gorilla trekking expedition in Uganda to an educational program connecting international institutions with African expertise, our approach remains the same.

Why Vild exists

Access should come with trust.

Finding an experience is one thing. Knowing who to trust, what to expect and how everything will come together is another. We exist to close that gap.

Our role is to make complex experiences and programs easier to discover, plan and coordinate, while building meaningful connections with the African businesses, professionals and institutions that make them possible.

We don't believe in making promises simply to win business. We believe in understanding expectations, setting clear responsibilities and doing the work required to deliver.

Our journey

Rooted in Uganda. Looking across Africa.

Uganda is where our journey begins. Its extraordinary biodiversity, wildlife, landscapes and people provide the foundation for our initial experiences and partnerships.

We are building our understanding, developing our operating relationships and establishing standards that can support long-term growth. Our geographical ambition extends beyond Uganda, across East Africa and eventually into other African markets—but expansion is not simply about reaching more destinations. It is about ensuring that the quality, relationships and accountability associated with Vild remain consistent wherever we operate.

Our vision

A more connected Africa.

We envision an internationally respected African company connecting people, institutions and businesses with the experiences, expertise and opportunities that make Africa extraordinary.

Our ambition is to build a lasting network founded on trust, meaningful partnerships and professional execution. We are not interested in becoming the biggest simply for the sake of size. We want to become a company whose name means something.
