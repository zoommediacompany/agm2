About WILD

Connecting Africa with the world.

WILD is building a trusted network connecting international students, universities, professionals, researchers and travellers with African institutions, organisations and experiences.

Our vision

To become Africa’s most trusted international mobility and experience network.

Our mission

To make Africa professionally accessible, academically valuable, safely navigable and genuinely discoverable.
